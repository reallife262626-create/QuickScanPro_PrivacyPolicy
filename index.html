4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • App Functionality: To enable document scanning, OCR text recognition (Hindi & English), and PDF creation.
  • Local Storage: All scanned documents and images are stored exclusively on your device. We do not upload or transmit your scans to any server.
  • Advertising: To display relevant advertisements through Google AdMob, which helps keep the App free to use.
  • App Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve the App's performance and user experience.
  • Technical Support: To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support when you contact us.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your privacy and data security are our top priorities:

  • Local Storage Only: All scanned documents, images, and OCR results are stored locally on your device using Android's internal storage. No data is transmitted to our servers.
  • No Cloud Storage: We do not offer or use any cloud storage service. Your documents never leave your device.
  • No Server Communication: The App does not communicate with any of our servers. The only internet connection made is to Google AdMob for loading advertisements.
  • Data Encryption: Data transmitted between the App and Google AdMob is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS encryption.
  • No Permanent Storage by Us: We have no access to your scanned documents or personal files at any time.

9. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Delete: You can delete all scanned documents directly from within the App. Since all data is stored locally on your device, uninstalling the App will also permanently delete all associated data.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Ads: You can opt out of personalized advertising by going to your device Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID.
  • Right to Revoke Permissions: You can revoke Camera permission at any time through your device Settings > Apps > QuickScanPro > Permissions.
  • Right to Access: Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full access to all your scanned documents at all times.
  • Right to Information: You can contact us at any time at cricpromulti@gmail.com to inquire about what data we collect.

10. Data Retention

Since all scanned documents and user data are stored locally on your device, data retention is entirely under your control:
